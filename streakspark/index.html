StreakSpark

Small steps. Lasting change.

A Christian habit app for iPhone that I designed, built and run on my own.

Visit the StreakSpark site

In TestFlight beta now, coming to the App Store.

Build habits, and break them.

A habit you are building says Done. A habit you are breaking says Stayed clear, and it lets you be honest with I slipped. On a break habit you can note what triggered it, so the pattern becomes visible.

Streaks that know your schedule.

Rest days are rest days. A streak only counts the days you planned, so a Sunday off never costs you. You can set a day goal for any habit and see when you will reach it.

A reset is part of the process.

Miss a day and StreakSpark does not scold you. It asks what got in the way and what would make tomorrow easier. Then you carry on.

The star on the Goals screen grows every day for the first 30 days, then at 50, 100, 200 and 365. Try it.

A 1-day streak. The star grows again at 2 days.

One verse, every day.

Each day opens with one verse from the World English Bible, a public-domain translation. Everyone sees the same verse that day.
